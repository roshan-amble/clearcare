# ClearCare terms of use

ClearCare is open-source software that a family runs on its own computer to keep one person's health record. These
terms apply to anyone who connects their patient-portal account (such as MyChart) to a ClearCare installation.

## What you agree to

- **You authorize read-only access.** When you connect your patient-portal account, you allow that ClearCare
  installation to read your health records: problems, medications, allergies, immunizations, results, imaging
  reports, visit notes and visit summaries. ClearCare never writes to, changes or deletes anything in your portal.
- **You can stop at any time.** Disconnect from ClearCare's Settings page, or revoke ClearCare's access from your
  patient portal's account settings. The family running the installation can also delete everything ClearCare stored.
- **Who can see your data.** Only you and the family members you approve, through a private network connection to
  the family's own computer. ClearCare has no public website and no shared server.

## What ClearCare is not

- **It is not a medical device and not medical advice.** ClearCare includes an AI assistant that can explain and
  organize your records, and it can be wrong. Always follow the advice of your doctors. In an emergency, call 911.
- **It is not a hospital or doctor service.** Your health system does not run ClearCare and is not responsible for it.
- **It comes with no warranty.** The software is provided as is, without guarantees of accuracy or availability.
