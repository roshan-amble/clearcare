# ClearCare privacy policy

ClearCare is open-source software that one family runs on its own computer to keep one person's health record. There
is no company behind it, no shared server, and no account with anyone but your own patient portal.

## What ClearCare keeps

- **From your patient portal**, only with your permission: problems, medications, allergies, immunizations, lab and
  test results, imaging reports, visit notes and visit summaries.
- **What you and your family enter**: appointments, symptoms, weight, meals, questions for your doctors, uploaded
  documents, and chats with the assistant.

## Where it is kept

Everything is stored on the family's own computer, in a folder only that computer's account can read. It is protected
by the computer's disk encryption (FileVault on a Mac). The keys that let ClearCare read your portal are kept in the
computer's keychain. Nothing is stored on a ClearCare server, because there is none.

## Who can see it

Only you and the family members you approve, through a private network (Tailscale) that connects your devices to the
family's computer. Each person signs in with their own account, and only people on the approved list get in.

## Who it is shared with

- **The AI service the family chose**, only when you or your family ask the assistant a question. Your record is sent
  with that question so the assistant can answer it. The service is chosen by the family in ClearCare's settings
  (for example OpenAI through the family's ChatGPT account, or Anthropic); that service's own data terms apply.
- **No one else.** Your data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with data brokers. ClearCare
  has no analytics or tracking.

## How long it is kept, and how to remove it

Your data stays until it is deleted. You can disconnect your patient portal at any time from ClearCare's Settings page
or from your portal's account settings, which stops any further reading. The family running the installation can
delete individual records, uploaded documents, or the whole record.

## Questions

Ask the family member who runs your ClearCare installation. Questions about the software itself can be raised in the
project's public repository.
